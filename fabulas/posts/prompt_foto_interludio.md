# Prompts para imagem — Interludio: O Dia que Ninguem Fez

## Contexto
Bestiario real do dia 09/06/2026 (Florianopolis): gaviao-carijo filmado na arvore,
pica-pau de cabeca amarela, gralha-azul, bem-te-vi fazendo backflip no acai,
beija-flor, aracua, duas mariposas, borboleta amarela, urubu, fragatas.
A imagem do post deve contrastar com fauna renderizada/artificial — aqui e fauna VIVA,
observada, de campo. O estilo e o mesmo DNA dos posts anteriores: storybook, watercolor,
muted earth tones, hand-painted warmth.

---

## Prompt 1 — Principal (bestiario vivo + casa + espelho)

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: a rustic Brazilian coastal house on a rainy morning. The front porch has a worn wooden railing. An elderly woman sits in a wicker chair, peeling an orange with an old knife — the peel falls in one long unbroken spiral. She looks calm, unhurried, wise.

In the doorway, a small muscular puppy (brown, compact, American Staffordshire type) lies on a simple dog bed, nose tucked between paws, sleeping soundly despite distant thunder.

The magic is OUTSIDE the house — the yard and trees are alive with real birds, all painted with naturalistic detail but storybook warmth:

- A crested caracara (gaviao-carijo) perched on a branch of a tall tree, looking down with sharp golden eyes
- A yellow-headed woodpecker (pica-pau) tapping against a trunk, mid-percussion
- A bright blue jay-like bird (gralha-azul, Cyanocorax caeruleus) on a wire, head tilted
- A great kiskadee (bem-te-vi) mid-backflip catching a small dark acai berry in the air — dynamic, joyful
- A hummingbird (beija-flor) hovering near a flower on the porch railing
- Two small moths (mariposas) resting on the window frame
- A single yellow butterfly (borboleta amarela) floating in the foreground, closest to the viewer

On the wall inside, barely visible through the door: a simple mirror reflecting an empty room. Clean. Waiting.

Rain falls softly. The rooster (galo) stands in the yard, crowing — wet feathers, head up, singing anyway.

The overall mood: the house is quiet, the world outside is alive with real creatures doing real things. No screens. No interfaces. Just observation, patience, and a rooster that never misses his shift.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted watercolor, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: warm ambers, rain grays, soft greens, muted blues, flashes of yellow (butterfly, woodpecker crest, kiskadee belly). Morning light filtering through clouds.

## Prompt 2 — Minimalista (galo na chuva)

A single rooster standing in the rain, watercolor style. Head up, crowing. Feathers soaked, crest pressed down by water. Behind him, a blurred coastal Brazilian house with warm light in the windows. A yellow butterfly floats past, unbothered by the rain. One small brown puppy visible through the doorway, sleeping on a bed. The feeling: duty doesn't wait for good weather. Storybook illustration, muted earth tones, hand-painted warmth, soft rain texture. No text. Aspect ratio 4:5.

---

## Prompt 3 — Alternativo (bem-te-vi backflip)

A great kiskadee bird (bem-te-vi, bright yellow belly, black eye stripe) caught mid-air doing a backflip to catch a small dark berry from an acai palm. Dynamic pose, wings spread, pure joy. Background: soft-focus Brazilian coastal vegetation, morning light, hints of rain. A blue jay (gralha-azul) watches from a wire above. A yellow butterfly crosses the frame. Watercolor storybook style, warm earth tones, hand-painted feel. No text. Aspect ratio 4:5.

---

## Notas de uso
- Prompt 1: imagem principal do post (hero). Mostra o mundo todo do interludio.
- Prompt 2: imagem de apoio ou stories. O galo na chuva = a frase de fecho do post.
- Prompt 3: imagem alternativa se quiser destaque na fauna. O backflip do bem-te-vi e a cena mais viva do dia.
- Hashtag #Fable no post e intencional e sutil. Quem sabe, sabe.
- As imagens sao de FAUNA REAL observada em campo, nao renderizada pra efeito. Esse e o ponto.
