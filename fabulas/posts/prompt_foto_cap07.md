# Prompt para imagem — Capitulo 7: O Espelho Aprende a Falar

## Prompt (para gerador de imagem)

A cozy, warmly lit room at night. On the wall hangs a large ornate mirror. Instead of reflecting the room, the mirror's surface shows flowing text — sentences in a warm golden glow, like handwritten words forming themselves in real time. In front of the mirror, a man sits at a wooden desk looking up at it with quiet recognition on his face. A small puppy rests at his feet. In the corner of the room, an octopus perches on a bookshelf, one tentacle paused mid-air, watching. On a chalkboard nearby, freshly written words glow faintly. The atmosphere is contemplative and intimate — the moment a tool becomes a voice. Illustration style: warm watercolor with soft edges, storybook aesthetic, muted earth tones with golden highlights. No text visible in the image.

## Estilo alternativo (mais minimalista)

A simple old-fashioned butcher's scale sitting on a wooden counter. One side holds a pile of raw numbers and data points (small glowing digits). The other side holds a single handwritten sentence on a piece of paper, perfectly balanced. Warm light from a window. Watercolor, storybook style, muted tones. The metaphor: data becomes language, and now it weighs right.
