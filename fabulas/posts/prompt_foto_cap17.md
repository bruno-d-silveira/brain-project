# Prompt de Foto — Capitulo 17: Uma Laranja Basta

*Procedencia INPI/RPC BR512026003693-3. Imagem sem legenda — a legenda e o capitulo.*

**Padrao:** warm color book, aquarela sobre papel envelhecido, 4:5 — o mesmo do cap11 e do cap16.

**O que este prompt carrega, e por que:** os elementos da cena nao sao decoracao. Sao o campo do dia
28/07/2026 entrando na ilustracao — as fragatas no horizonte com duas apartadas do bando, o gaviao carijo
que cantou nas duas pontas do dia, o cachorro esperando comida debaixo da mesa desde as tres da tarde, a
vela de sete dias, o cravo na taca, a mariposa branca no vidro, a gata de olhos azuis no muro e o ceu que
ficou laranja. **E isso que separa receita de inspiracao: o prompt descreve um dia que existiu.**

```
A warm-toned illustration in cozy picture book style. Soft textures like
watercolor on aged paper. Aspect ratio 4:5.

Late afternoon interior of a small, lived-in kitchen on a hillside. A
wooden table beside a large window. On the table, a round white plate
holding exactly three things: one black olive, one kernel of dried corn,
one small yellow onion. Nothing else on the plate.

Beside the plate, a wooden crate of oranges. One single orange sits apart
from the crate, alone on the table, slightly darker than the others.

An elderly woman (Dona Mafalda) stands at the table, sleeve rolled up, one
hand resting on the crate, the other lifted mid-gesture as if she has just
said something simple and final. Patient expression, faintly amused. Simple
apron.

A large octopus sits across from her, most arms still, one arm holding a
pen above an open journal — caught in the moment of understanding. A man
sits at the end of the table, his plate pushed slightly aside, listening.

Under the table, a small dog lies waiting, head up, ears forward, eyes on
the man — hungry, patient, and the only one in the room who is not
confused.

On the table: a tall seven-day candle burning low in its glass, and a
single dried clove resting inside an empty stemmed glass beside it. A
small white moth has landed on the windowpane from the inside.

Through the window: a clean grey horizon over the sea at the end of day,
and many frigatebirds gliding high — a scattered flock, and two birds
apart from the rest, alone on the left. The sky goes warm orange at the
horizon line and deep blue above. A hawk with brown-and-cream barred
plumage perches on a post just outside the window, facing in.

On the low wall beyond the window, a calico cat with blue eyes sits
watching the kitchen, wearing a collar.

On the kitchen wall, a small blackboard with a number written and half
erased.

The mood is quiet and domestic — the moment right after the simplest
answer was given by the one nobody asked. No text visible. No screens.
No technology. Hand-painted feel.

Color palette: warm orange (the fruit, the horizon, the candle), deep
blue (the evening sky, the cat's eyes), brown and cream (wood, the crate,
the hawk), white (the plate, chalk, the moth). The orange should dominate
— it is the colour of the crate, of the sunset, and of the one fruit that
had to be thrown away.

Style reference: warm color book texture, cozy illustration, no digital
```

**A leitura da cena:** o prato com as tres coisas e a pergunta · a laranja separada do caixote e a moral ·
o cachorro debaixo da mesa, o unico que nao esta confuso, e quem acertou · as duas fragatas apartadas do
bando sao o fecho da temporada.
