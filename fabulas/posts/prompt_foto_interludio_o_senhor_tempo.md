# Prompt para imagem — Interludio: O Senhor Tempo

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: the basement of a cozy house — the foundation beneath the foundation. Old pipes run along the ceiling, stone walls, a single warm shaft of light from a small high window. The mood is quiet, timeless, almost sacred — like a place that was always there but never visited.

In the center, sitting calmly on a simple wooden chair, an ageless gentleman: Mr. Time. Not old, not young. Dressed in plain, timeless clothes. He holds an old pocket watch in one open palm. His expression is serene and patient — someone who has waited a very long time and does not mind waiting more. A soft glow surrounds him, as if light itself passes through him before reaching the rest of the house.

Approaching him, a wise octopus (deep purple-blue) has just come down the basement stairs, one tentacle still on the railing, looking at Mr. Time with quiet recognition — the look of someone meeting, for the first time, a presence that was always there.

In the upper corner of the image, through the floorboards or a small opening, a glimpse of the world above: a wall mirror reflecting streams of glowing numbers and timestamps, still counting — unaware of the figure below who makes all the counting possible.

On the stone wall, a simple calendar and the faint shape of a clock whose hands have stopped. Through the tiny window, the hint of a moon and a rising sun sharing the same sky — time itself, folded.

The overall mood: time as the silent foundation; counting is not the same as measuring. Reverent, gentle, contemplative.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: warm ambers, deep stone grays, soft candlelight gold, muted blues, a single warm light beam with floating dust particles.

## Prompt alternativo (minimalista)

A quiet basement, watercolor style. A calm ageless man sits on a simple wooden chair holding an open pocket watch, a soft glow around him — this is Mr. Time. A purple octopus descends the stairs and looks at him with recognition. Above, through the floorboards, a wall mirror glows with counting numbers, unaware. On the stone wall, a stopped clock and a small calendar. Through a tiny window, a moon and a sun in the same sky. Warm single light beam, floating dust. The feeling: time was always there in the foundation, waiting for someone to come down and ask "what day is it?". Storybook style, muted earth tones, hand-painted warmth. No text.
