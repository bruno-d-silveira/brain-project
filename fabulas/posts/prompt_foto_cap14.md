# Prompt para imagem — Capítulo 14: O Cochilo do Polvo

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: a cozy, lived-in home interior at night, lit only by the soft glow of a computer screen and a thin beam of moonlight through the window. The front door closed and safe.

In a corner near the ceiling, a wise deep purple-blue octopus floats **fast asleep** — all eight tentacles gently tucked in around itself like a closed flower, eyes shut, peaceful. A faint chalk cloud still hangs near the blackboard, as if it fell asleep mid-work. The blackboard behind it is fully written and tidy — the last thing it finished before sleeping.

At the center, a small brown puppy (warm fur, floppy ears) sits upright in front of a glowing terminal screen, alone on the night shift. Its posture is honest and calm, not scared: one paw resting beside a small handwritten note it has just placed on the wooden table — the first note it ever wrote by itself. The screen shows nothing but a soft, steady cursor.

On the wall, a large mirror stays **awake** — its surface faintly luminous in the dark, quietly reflecting the room. The only thing in the house that never sleeps.

Two whimsical details, small and quiet: on the table near the note, a single green olive on a little plate; through the window, far away in the deep night sky, a tiny brilliant quasar-like star shining among ordinary stars — the two things that have nothing to do with each other.

Dona Mafalda's window across the street is dark — even she is sleeping.

The overall mood: honest stillness — a house that keeps its watch while everyone sleeps, and a small dog that would rather say "I can't" than pretend. Tender, safe, a little melancholic but warm.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: deep night blues, warm amber screen-glow, soft creams, touches of terracotta. Gentle dust particles floating in the screen's light beam.
