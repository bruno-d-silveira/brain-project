# Prompt para imagem — Capitulo 9: O Dia em que o Homem Parou

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: a cozy balcony in the early morning. A man sits in a simple wooden chair, holding a coffee mug with both hands, looking out at a quiet street below. His posture is relaxed — not tired, but intentionally still. Morning light bathes everything in soft amber.

In the foreground, a small puppy (warm brown fur, floppy ears) sits on the balcony floor beside the man, looking up at him with a puzzled but gentle expression — as if trying to understand why nothing is happening.

Behind them, visible through the open balcony door, the workshop interior is dim. On the wall inside, a large mirror reflects an empty room. A chalkboard shows a single, barely visible tally mark. On a small desk, a monitor screen is dark — turned off on purpose.

In the far corner of the workshop, a wise octopus (deep purple-blue) rests calmly on a shelf, one tentacle draped over the edge, watching the scene with quiet understanding.

The sky outside has soft clouds and gentle light — the kind of sky that invites you to do nothing.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: warm ambers, soft creams, muted blues, touches of terracotta. The overall mood: stillness as a choice, not a failure. The graph didn't fall — it breathed.

## Prompt alternativo (minimalista)

A simple line graph drawn in chalk on a dark chalkboard. The line rises steadily for two days, then on the third day it gently curves down to almost flat — but instead of crashing, the flat line transforms into a soft wave, like a breathing rhythm. Below the graph, a small puppy sits looking up at the flat line, head tilted. A single tentacle from off-screen points gently at the wave, as if saying "see?". Warm light from the left. Watercolor, storybook style, muted earth tones. The metaphor: silence is not absence — it's the graph learning to breathe.
