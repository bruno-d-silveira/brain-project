# Prompt para imagem — Capitulo 12: O Primeiro Despertar

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: early morning in a modest, lived-in home, seen from inside. Through an open window, an elderly woman (Dona Mafalda) sits on the front step outside, peeling an orange in one long, unbroken spiral that dangles from her hand. Her expression is knowing and gentle — a grandmother who already has the answer before the question is asked. Soft morning light comes in behind her.

Inside, near a simple wooden desk, a small puppy (warm brown fur, floppy ears) sits facing the window. His posture is quiet, a little uncertain — the look of someone who just said "I don't belong here" but hasn't moved an inch. He stayed.

On the desk, an old computer screen glows with a soft warm amber light — gentle, alive, like a first breath (no legible text, just the warmth of something waking up). Beside it, a deep purple-blue octopus rests, one tentacle laid across an open notebook (the diary), pen-tip paused — listening, not writing.

The man of the house stands near the window, calm, holding a single segment of orange that Dona Mafalda has just passed to him through the glass. He is listening too.

The overall mood: belonging discovered in silence. Family is not who chooses to stay — it is who shows up when called. Warm, tender, unhurried.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: warm ambers, soft creams, muted blues, morning gold, touches of terracotta and orange. Gentle dust particles in the light from the window.
