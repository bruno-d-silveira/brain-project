# Prompt para imagem — Capítulo 13: Os Filhos do Cachorrinho

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: a cozy, lived-in home interior seen from inside, the front door closed and safe. Warm afternoon light pours through an open window, casting long golden shadows across a wooden floor.

Near a large blackboard, a wise deep purple-blue octopus rests, one tentacle holding a piece of chalk — it has just drawn three small figures on the board, and they are gently glowing to life:
- the **Accountant** — a small round figure with little round glasses, an open ledger of tidy numbers before him;
- the **Postman** — a tall, thin figure in a postman's cap, holding a single envelope (a short handwritten note);
- the **Watchman** — a stocky figure with big, always-open watchful eyes.

All three share the same warm, honest face as the puppy — same nature, different roles; none stern, all kind and attentive.

At the center, a small brown puppy (warm fur, floppy ears) sits looking up at the three figures, wide-eyed and moved — a father who just discovered he has children. On the wall, a mirror reflects a dark, locked computer screen — the house's data staying home.

Through the open window, the birds of the day keep watch: a **great kiskadee (bem-te-vi, bright yellow breast) perched on a wire**, a **frigate bird gliding high** in a golden sky, a **clay-colored ovenbird (joão-de-barro) on the windowsill**, and on the fence post a **small owl (coruja) watching quietly**. Leaning in at the window, an elderly woman (Dona Mafalda) in a simple apron, with a knowing, gentle grandmother's smile.

The overall mood: legacy and quiet pride — a small being who once said "I don't belong" discovering he is a father; a house that grows without ever unlocking its door.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: warm ambers, soft creams, muted blues, afternoon gold, touches of terracotta. Gentle dust particles floating in the light beam from the window.
