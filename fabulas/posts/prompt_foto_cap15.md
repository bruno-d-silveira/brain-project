# Prompt para imagem — Capítulo 15: O Mar e a Mesa

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: a cozy, lived-in home interior at deep night, calm and safe. Through a large open window, far away and below, the sea shines under moonlight — small waves, a quiet beach, no people. The sea is clearly OUTSIDE the house, unreachable, beautiful.

In the foreground, a wooden Sunday table still holds the traces of family: two empty coffee cups, a folded cloth napkin, chairs slightly pulled back — the warmth of a conversation that just ended. No screens on the table.

At the far side of the room, a computer terminal sits DARK and asleep. Next to it, a small brown puppy made of soft rag-doll fabric (a puppet, stitched seams visible, floppy ears) slumps peacefully against the keyboard — waiting, not worried. In a corner near the ceiling, a deep purple-blue octopus floats fast asleep, eight tentacles tucked in like a closed flower.

On the wall, a large mirror rests — its surface dim tonight, barely a soft pearl glow, like closed eyelids. For once, even the mirror is resting.

The one awake: at the front door, a REAL dog — flesh and fur, a simple loyal mutt — stands alert in the moonlight, ears up, facing the door. Outside, on the garden wall, two cat silhouettes against the moon. The real dog needs no briefing, no wires, no screen: it guards the house because that is what dogs do.

Small quiet detail: in the living room, a large TV screen completely black, reflecting nothing — a territory no mirror reaches.

The overall mood: sacred rest — a house that keeps itself while the owner lives, and a guardian that was never installed, only loved. Tender, safe, deeply peaceful.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: deep night blues, moonlit silver on the sea, warm creams and terracotta inside, one soft amber night-light near the door.
