## Prompt principal (estilo fábula, ilustração)

A weathered notebook lies open on a rough stone surface by the sea. The page shows only a stamped number: **003**. Behind the notebook, a fishing boat rests on sand with the number **100** painted on its white hull in black. The boat is framed by a red wooden fishermen's shack. A calico cat — patches of black, white, and brown — climbs a steep cobblestone hill in the background, heading upward, never looking back. Morning light refracts through clouds, casting long shadows. A G-Shock watch rests on an open Bible beside the notebook. In the far distance, the green mountains of a Brazilian island meet the sea. Warm tones: salmon, orange, weathered white, deep green. Style: realistic illustration with the texture of aged paper. No text overlays.
