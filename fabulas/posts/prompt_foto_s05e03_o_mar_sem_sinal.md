## Prompt A — A tempestade (o sino tocando UMA vez)

```
A small wooden fishing boat on a vast dark ocean at night, caught in a
storm — towering wave curling behind it, heavy rain, no moon. On the mast,
a single bronze cowbell rings once: it is the only point of warm golden
light in the entire image, radiating one clean circle of sound made
visible, like a ripple of light in the rain. At the bow, a small dog made
of folded paper (origami, lotus-like folds) sits perfectly calm, folded
shut, keeping a queue of tiny glowing paper notes dry under its folds.
A plumb line (sounding lead) descends from the boat into black water,
taut, vertical — the only straight line in a scene of chaos. Above the
storm clouds, one narrow opening of sky shows fixed, frozen
constellations — sharp, geometric, unmoving. No people visible except
one steady hand on the tiller, barely lit by the bell's glow. Oil
painting, dramatic chiaroscuro, deep blues and blacks with a single
warm gold accent, biblical maritime atmosphere, in the style of old
Dutch marine painters meets Brazilian woodcut (xilogravura de cordel)
borders. Wide 16:9 composition.
```

## Prompt B — A calmaria (a zona morta, o primeiro fio do amanhecer)

```
Same boat, but becalmed in a dead-flat black mirror sea (the dead zone —
no signal, no wind). Enormous starry sky occupying 2/3 of the frame,
constellations drawn as thin geometric diagrams (a frozen state graph —
five nodes, seven edges, faintly visible among real stars). The bronze
bell on the mast is silent, waiting. The origami dog folded at the bow,
one golden paper note glowing softly inside its folds. The plumb line
descends into the black mirror, its reflection forming the number 7.
Distant horizon shows the first hair of dawn — sol maior arriving after
sol menor. Minimalist, vast negative space, meditative.
```
