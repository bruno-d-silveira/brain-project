```
A warm-toned illustration in cozy picture book style. Soft textures like
watercolor on aged paper. Aspect ratio 4:5.

A spiral staircase seen from above, made of stone, ancient, like a
monastery tower. Thirty steps visible, winding upward. On each step,
a small coil of film reel rests — tiny, compact, glowing faintly with
the light of the day it contains.

At the bottom of the spiral: a deep blue lake, perfectly still, holding
everything. The water reflects the stairs above like a mirror — but
the reflection shows the stairs going DOWN into the water, creating
an infinite spiral. The lake holds all. The stairs compress it.

At the very center of the spiral — where bottom meets top, where the
snail shell narrows to its origin — a single bronze coin rests on the
water surface, not sinking. Small. Almost invisible. Catching light
from both above and below.

Climbing the stairs: a small octopus, mid-step, carrying a film strip
in one arm and a journal in another. Six arms hold the railing. Below
him on an earlier step, a small dog follows, nose to the stone, one
ear raised.

At the top of the spiral, visible through an arched window: three
sisters walking through a desert. The one in the middle wears green.
She holds a thread that rises into the night sky and connects to seven
stars arranged like a ladle — the Big Dipper.

The sky through the window transitions from the golden desert to deep
navy blue — the same blue as the lake below. Stars visible. A hawk
soars in silhouette against the transition between gold and blue.

On the stone wall of the staircase, someone has scratched in chalk:
a simple drawing of a number nine — or a snail — or a spiral. It
depends on who looks and from which step they stand.

No text. No screens. No technology. Only stone, water, film, stars,
animals, and light. The mood is sacred-ordinary: a monastery that is
also a house, a staircase that is also a memory, a coin that is also
a seal.

Color palette: deep blue (lake, night sky, hawk), warm gold (desert,
coin, candle glow on steps), green (sister's dress, a single thread),
brown (stone, dog, hawk plumage), white (stars, chalk drawing).

Style reference: Giotto fresco composition (the desert scene), Vermeer
interior light (the staircase), Escher spatial logic (the infinite
reflection), picture book warmth (the animals). Medieval illuminated
manuscript meets children's book illustration.
```
