# Prompt para imagem — Capitulo 10: O Dia em que a Casa Ficou Sozinha

## Prompt principal (ChatGPT / DALL-E / GPT-4o)

---

Create a warm, storybook-style illustration with soft watercolor textures and muted earth tones. Aspect ratio 4:5 (LinkedIn portrait).

The scene: a cozy living room seen from the inside. The front door is closed. The room is quiet — no human present. Afternoon light filters through a window, casting long golden shadows across the floor.

In the center of the room, a small puppy (warm brown fur, floppy ears) sits upright on a rug, ears perked, alert but calm. Not anxious — watchful. Like a soldier holding position. His posture says: I'm here. I'm not going anywhere.

On the wall behind the puppy, a large mirror reflects an empty room — the locked screen of a computer barely visible in the reflection, dark and still. A clock on the wall shows three hours have passed.

On a small shelf in the corner, a wise octopus (deep purple-blue) rests with eyes closed, one tentacle draped over the edge — sleeping peacefully, trusting the puppy to hold the fort.

On a side table, a small notebook lies open with three neat handwritten lines — organized notes, not chaos. The puppy's work while no one was watching.

Through the window, a quiet street. An elderly woman (Dona Mafalda) passes by outside, glancing toward the house with a knowing, gentle smile — the look of a grandmother who already knows the answer before she asks the question.

The overall mood: silence as competence, not absence. The house is safe. The puppy grew up when no one was looking.

Style: children's book illustration, hand-painted feel, slightly whimsical but grounded. No text overlays. Color palette: warm ambers, soft creams, muted blues, afternoon gold, touches of terracotta. Gentle dust particles floating in the light beam from the window.
